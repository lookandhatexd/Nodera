# Tool logo sources

The provider logos embedded in `Nodera.py` were retrieved on 2026-09-05 from
the following SVG libraries. Their path geometry is preserved. Integration
changes are limited to sizing, accessibility attributes, white monochrome
presentation on the dark background, and unique Gemini gradient IDs.

- OpenAI: [SVGL source](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/openai.svg).
- Anthropic: [Lobe Icons source](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/anthropic.svg).
- Gemini: [Lobe Icons source](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/gemini-color.svg).

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini marks belong to their respective owners. These
libraries distribute the SVG files; they are not the brand owners. The generic
Open models and Custom agents glyphs are project UI icons, not brand logos.
